# Code of conduct

This project exists to help people work together peacefully. These commitments apply in issues, discussions, pull requests and any space that carries the project's name.

## What we ask of everyone

1. **Peaceful methods only.** Work through voluntary, democratic, commercial, diplomatic and civic means. Do not propose, plan or encourage violence, intimidation, sabotage or illegal action.
2. **Treat people with respect.** Argue with ideas, not with people. No harassment, slurs, threats or personal attacks.
3. **Welcome people at every level.** A first-time neighbour and an energy minister are both welcome here. Explain things plainly and be generous with newcomers.
4. **Protect privacy.** Do not share anyone's private details. Share a city or region, never a home address.
5. **Be honest.** Do not invent numbers, impersonate others or inflate reports. Say when you are unsure.
6. **Disagree openly and kindly.** Many questions in the plan are contested, such as the role of markets or nuclear power. Make your case, listen to others, and accept that reasonable people differ.

## Reporting a problem

Contact a maintainer through a private channel listed in the repository's profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the item. Please do not argue about enforcement in public threads.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, lock threads, and block people who break these commitments. They will explain the reason where they can, and decisions can be raised with another maintainer.
