        How it works

        1. Pick a task. Choose one at your scale from the list above, or propose a new one.
        2. Claim it. Comment /claim on the issue so others know. Comment /release if you need to step back.
        3. Do it, and take others with you. Ask a neighbour, a school or a council to join in. Share what you learn in the issue.
        4. Report it. File a report with the numbers. A maintainer checks it and adds the verified label.

        Ground rules

        • Peaceful methods only: voluntary, democratic, commercial, diplomatic or civic.
        • Public by default, private where it counts. Share a city or region, never a home address or anyone's personal details.
        • Honest numbers. Every figure needs a source or a note on how it was worked out.
        • Disagree openly and kindly. Many choices in the plan, such as how to fund a basic floor or what role nuclear power should play, are contested.
