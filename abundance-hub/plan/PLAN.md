# The Abundance Plan (summary)

A peaceful path to a world where everyone's needs are met. This is the short version that the hub is built around. If you have the full plan document, save it next to this file as `plan/FULL-PLAN.md` and link to it from here.

Targets and baselines are proposals and approximate figures. Check each against current IEA, IRENA, World Bank, WHO, FAO and UN data before using it in public planning, and correct anything wrong with a "Correct a number" issue.

## The idea

In Star Trek, humanity moves from a society organised around accumulating wealth to one where nobody goes without. That story has three ingredients: abundant energy, a shared sense of purpose, and institutions that make cooperation easier than conflict. Replicators do not exist, and Trek's utopia grew out of a catastrophe. This plan tries to get the same benefits with real technology and without waiting for a disaster.

1. **An energy system** that makes power abundant, clean and cheap.
2. **A Needs Floor** so that everyone has food, water, shelter, energy, health care, education, connection, mobility, economic security and safety.
3. **A cooperation system** of habits, institutions and agreements that make peaceful collaboration the easy path.

## Principles

1. No violence as a method. Every tool is voluntary, democratic, commercial, diplomatic or civic.
2. Energy is the lever; people are the point.
3. Floor before ceiling: guarantee a decent minimum first.
4. Do what already works. Treat speculative technology such as fusion as upside, not a dependency.
5. Local ownership: communities that own their systems defend and improve them.
6. Measure and adjust in public.
7. Fairness in transition: people and places that lose from change get real support.
8. Honest disagreement. Many choices are contested and the plan names the trade-offs.

## The energy system

| Layer | What it is |
|---|---|
| Efficiency first | Insulation, heat pumps, efficient appliances and motors. The cheapest energy is the energy not used. |
| Variable renewable backbone | Solar and wind, with batteries for daily balancing and long-duration storage for multi-day gaps. |
| Firm clean power | Geothermal, hydro and nuclear fission where each fits the region. |
| Grids and flexibility | Long-distance transmission, smarter grids, flexible demand, links between regions. |
| Electrification and clean fuels | Electric transport and heating; green hydrogen and synthetic fuels for steel, shipping, aviation and fertiliser. |
| Local resilience | Rooftop and community solar, batteries and microgrids for clinics, schools and shelters. |
| Materials and recycling | Recycling, diversified mineral supply and lower-mineral chemistries. |
| Research bets | Open public and private research into advanced geothermal, storage, fission and fusion. |

Evidence behind it, as reported in 2026: solar and storage costs have fallen sharply since 2010 (IRENA), round-the-clock solar-plus-storage is now competitive with fossil power in sunny regions, grid battery capacity grew more than twelvefold between 2020 and 2024 (IEA), and fusion remains promising but unproven commercially.

## Roadmap

| Phase | Years | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Foundations | Late 2026 to 2027 | Form local groups, publish baselines, launch easy wins, start cross-border conversations. |
| 1 Deploy what works | 2027 to 2030 | Triple renewable capacity, expand storage, connect the people without power, retrofit homes, fund transition support. |
| 2 Firm power and cooperation | 2030 to 2040 | Add geothermal, long-duration storage and advanced nuclear; electrify transport, heating and industry; link grids across borders. |
| 3 Abundance and sharing | 2040 to 2055 | Very high clean electricity; move savings into housing, health, education and science; keep institutions fair. |

## Work at every scale

- **Person:** learn your numbers, cut waste, know your neighbours, share a skill, take part locally.
- **Household:** audit, insulate, electrify, add solar, cut food waste, make an emergency plan.
- **Neighbourhood:** start a group, buy together, repair and share, run a resilience hub, grow and share food, set up mutual aid.
- **City or region:** modern building codes, retrofit programmes, grid planning, transit, zoning reform, workforce training.
- **Nation:** an energy plan with a schedule, permitting reform, an income or services floor, open research, just transition.
- **Global:** cross-border grids, a critical-minerals compact, energy-access finance, nuclear safeguards, protection for civilian infrastructure, open technology, people-to-people ties.

Each of these is a starter task in `docs/data/tasks.json` and becomes a GitHub issue when you run the **Seed starter tasks** workflow.

## Risks to watch

Fusion or other new technology arriving late; concentration of mineral supply and manufacturing; benefits reaching some people and not others; attacks on grids and extreme weather; mining and land-use harm; rebound effects from cheaper energy; political polarisation; elites capturing the gains; conflict; and disinformation and volunteer burnout.
